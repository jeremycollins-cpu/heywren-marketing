The Follow-Through Problem for Founders

You context-switch between product, sales, hiring, fundraising, and operations every hour. Commitments scatter across every channel.

🎩
Wearing Too Many Hats
You make commitments as CEO, product lead, chief recruiter, and top salesperson — all in the same day. No human can track promises across that many contexts without things slipping.
🏛️
Board & Investor Commitments
Board meeting action items and investor follow-ups carry outsized consequences. A missed deliverable to your board erodes confidence at the worst possible level.
👥
Team Accountability Gaps
You delegate constantly but can't track everything. "Did marketing ship that launch plan?" and "Did we close the loop with that candidate?" pile up faster than you can check.

How HeyWren Helps Founders
