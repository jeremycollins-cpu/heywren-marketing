The Follow-Through Problem in Sales

Your team makes promises on every call. Without a system, the most important ones get lost in the shuffle.

📧
Proposals Not Sent on Time
"I'll get that proposal over by end of day" — then three deals compete for attention and the hottest lead goes cold. Delayed proposals signal disorganization to prospects.
📞
Follow-Ups Missed After Calls
Notes from discovery calls pile up while reps jump to the next meeting. Action items promised during calls — pricing details, references, technical specs — slip through the cracks.
🤝
Client Commitments Forgotten
When a rep promises to "loop in engineering" or "send updated terms," it lives in the prospect's memory even if it left the rep's. One broken promise can kill a deal.
