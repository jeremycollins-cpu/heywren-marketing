The Follow-Through Problem in Engineering

Your team makes dozens of commitments every week. Without a system, too many fall through the cracks.

💬
Lost in Slack Threads
Commitments made in fast-moving channels get buried. "I'll look into it" becomes forgotten by lunch. Important technical promises drown in thread noise.
🔄
Standup Follow-Ups Forgotten
Blockers raised at standup rarely get systematic follow-up. The same issues resurface sprint after sprint, eroding team trust and velocity.
🔗
Cross-Team Dependencies Dropped
API contracts, shared library updates, and deployment coordination between teams rely on memory. One missed handoff delays the entire release.

How HeyWren Helps Engineering Managers

🤖
Automatic Commitment Tracking
Wren's AI detects promises in Slack, email, and meetings — "I'll deploy by Friday," "Let me review that PR" — and tracks them without manual entry. Powered by Commitment Tracing.
🏃
Sprint-to-Sprint Follow-Through
Carry-over items and open commitments are surfaced at sprint boundaries. Know exactly what shipped, what slipped, and what needs attention — no manual audit needed.
🔍
Dependency Visibility
See cross-team promises in one view. When Platform owes Mobile an API by Wednesday, both teams see the commitment and get AI-powered nudges as the deadline approaches.
