How It Works

Wren uses natural language processing to identify commitments from your Slack messages, emails, and meeting notes. You can also tag @HeyWren in any message or add a commitment manually. Our AI learns your communication patterns over time.
Wren analyzes your messages to identify commitments and relationships, but we don't store them. Your raw message content never persists in our system. You control what channels Wren can access via Slack's permission model.
A nudge is a timely, contextual alert from Wren about a commitment that needs your attention—like an approaching deadline, a stakeholder waiting, or an unusual delay. Nudges show up in Slack and are action-ready: you can respond, delegate, or complete in seconds.
Commitment tracing follows a promise from its origin (meeting, email, or message) through every touchpoint until resolution. You see the full chain of context, who's involved, and what's blocking completion. Nothing falls through cracks.
Wren observes your execution patterns—how you handle commitments, your response time, meeting productivity, and more—and offers personalized coaching. Coaching is subtle: nudges, strategic reminders, and insights that help you improve without micromanaging.
The Draft Queue is a list of AI-generated follow-up messages that Wren pre-writes for you. Status updates, check-ins, reminders—Wren drafts them so you only review and send. Saves time and ensures nothing gets forgotten.
Before each meeting, Wren generates a briefing with open items, relationship health scores, and talking points based on your history with each attendee. You walk in prepared and meetings become more productive.
Tag @HeyWren in Slack or email to instantly flag any message or conversation for follow-through tracking. Wren will turn it into a commitment, trace it, and nudge you until it's done. A lightweight way to add context without leaving Slack.
Wren automatically identifies commitments owned by the person going out and initiates a handoff. You can reassign them, share context with a coverage person, or adjust timelines. Commitments don't disappear when your teammate goes offline.
