Questions? We Have Answers.

Is my data safe? How does Wren access my information? +

Wren uses read-only OAuth to connect to your Slack and email. We never store your messages—only analyze them to extract commitments. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We're SOC 2 compliant and never sell your data.

How is Wren different from project management tools? +

Project management tools require you to log commitments manually. Wren watches where you already work—Slack, email, meetings—and automatically captures everything you promise. It's designed for natural conversation, not rigid task workflows.

What tools does Wren connect to? +

Currently, Wren connects to Slack, Gmail, Outlook, and Google Calendar. We're building integrations with Microsoft Teams, Linear, Jira, and more. Let us know what you need.

How long does setup take? +

2 minutes. Sign up, authorize your tools with OAuth, and you're done. Wren starts learning immediately. You'll see your first nudges within the hour.

Can my team see my nudges and commitments? +

Your personal nudges are private by default. With Team plans, admins can set up shared dashboards to see team-wide follow-through metrics. Commitment details are never shared without your permission.

What happens after the free trial? +

At the end of your 7-day trial, you choose to upgrade to a paid plan or downgrade to Basic (free, limited features). We never charge without your explicit approval. Cancel anytime—no questions asked.

Can Wren help with team accountability? +

Yes. Team plans include team dashboards showing who's hitting their commitments, relationship health scores, and team-wide follow-through metrics. It's accountability without the micromanagement—people want to be reliable, Wren just makes it easy.
