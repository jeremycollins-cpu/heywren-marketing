HeyWren + Slack

Never Miss a Commitment in Slack Again

HeyWren integrates natively with Slack to automatically detect and track commitments made in channels, DMs, and threads. Using AI to understand natural conversation, Wren identifies promises, deadlines, and action items — then nudges you before they fall through the cracks. No manual logging required.

How It Works

1

Connect Slack via OAuth

One click to authorize. Wren gets read-only access to your workspace channels and DMs. No messages are stored — only commitments are extracted.

2

Wren reads your conversations

AI analyzes messages in real-time to detect commitments: "I'll send the proposal by Friday," "Let me follow up with the client," "I'll review the PR today."

3

Get nudges before things slip

When a commitment is approaching its deadline or at risk of being forgotten, Wren sends a gentle nudge right in Slack — where you already work.

Use Cases

Team Standups
Promises made in daily standups ("I'll finish the API endpoint today") are tracked automatically. No more forgotten follow-ups.
Client Promises
When someone tells a client "We'll have that ready by Thursday" in a shared channel, Wren ensures it actually happens.
Cross-Functional Requests
Requests that span teams ("Can design review this by EOD?") get tracked even when they'd normally fall between the cracks.
