Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 21, 2026

Introduction

HeyWren, Inc. ("HeyWren," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, including your name, email address, company name, and role. When you connect integrations (Slack, email, calendar), we process metadata about your communications to identify commitments and follow-through patterns.

We do not store the raw content of your messages, emails, or calendar events. We extract structured commitment data (who promised what, by when) and discard the original content.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate, maintain, and improve the HeyWren platform. This includes identifying commitments from your connected tools, generating AI nudges and follow-up suggestions, producing execution health metrics, and sending you product notifications.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. We may share data with service providers who help us operate HeyWren (hosting, analytics, email delivery), and we require them to protect your data.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256), row-level security in our database, and regular security audits. All data is hosted on SOC 2 compliant infrastructure.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Commitment and follow-through data is retained for 12 months of rolling history. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting privacy@heywren.ai.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can export your data at any time from your account settings. For GDPR, CCPA, or other privacy inquiries, contact us at privacy@heywren.ai.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@heywren.ai.
